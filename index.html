Training built around real clinical work

DentaPro Academy runs one-day courses for dental teams in Cairo and Alexandria. Every attendee practices on models, plans real cases with an instructor, and leaves with checklists and templates to use at the practice the next morning.

Our instructors are practicing specialists, so you learn the techniques and routines they use with patients every week.

  • Groups of 16 or fewer, so everyone gets time with the instructor.
  • A morning of case discussion, then an afternoon of hands-on practice on models.
  • Lunch, a printed workbook, and a certificate of attendance are included.
  • You take home checklists and templates for your own practice.

Who this is for

Clinicians

Dentists and specialists who want to add or refine implant, restorative, and surgical skills through hands-on practice with expert feedback. After a course, you can:

  • Plan and place an implant, from consultation to restoration.
  • Manage common complications before they become emergencies.
  • Photograph and document cases for referrals and patient records.
See clinical courses

Practice management

Owners and managers who want clearer treatment-plan presentations, better referral tracking, and a stronger team culture. After a course, you can:

  • Present treatment plans clearly and confidently.
  • Track every referral and follow up on time.
  • Run a daily huddle with clear roles for each team member.
See management courses

Team

Hygienists, assistants, and front-desk staff who help patients say yes to care and keep the day running smoothly. After a course, you can:

  • Explain procedures and costs in plain language.
  • Prepare rooms, instruments, and models for surgical days.
  • Handle patient questions and follow-up calls with confidence.
